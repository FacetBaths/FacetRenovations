## Why Visit a Home Show?

### Compare Multiple Companies
See how different renovation companies answer the same questions. You'll quickly figure out who knows what they're doing and who's winging it.

### See Materials in Person
Showroom samples under perfect lighting don't tell you what materials actually look like in a typical Chicago bathroom. Home show displays are more realistic.

### No Pressure Environment
Ask difficult questions. Walk away and think about it. Come back with more questions. Nobody's pressuring you to sign anything at a home show booth.

### Learn What You Don't Know
Half the battle in renovation planning is figuring out what questions to ask. Talking to multiple companies helps you understand what actually matters versus what's just sales talk.

## What to Bring

### Photos of Your Current Bathroom
Helps us understand your starting point and specific challenges. Phone photos are fine.

### Rough Measurements
Approximate bathroom dimensions help with ballpark pricing. Don't need precision—just length and width of the space.

### Your Questions List
Write down what you actually want to know. Easy to forget questions when you're talking to multiple vendors.

### Realistic Timeline
When do you want this done? Helps us discuss whether that timeline works with typical permit and material lead times.

## Other Reasons to Attend

### See What's Available Locally
Home shows bring together companies actually working in your area. Different than searching online and getting companies from Naperville trying to service Crystal Lake.

### Get Chicago-Specific Advice
Bathroom renovations in Chicago have unique challenges—permits, older building issues, climate considerations. Local companies understand these factors.

### Compare Process Approaches
Some companies handle everything. Some require you to source materials. Some provide fixed pricing. Some give estimates that change. Home shows let you compare approaches side by side.

## Questions We Hope You Ask

### "What's your typical payment structure?"
Reveals whether company is adequately capitalized or needs your deposit to finish someone else's job.

### "How do you handle timeline guarantees?"
Separates commitments from hopes.

### "What waterproofing systems do you use?"
Immediately shows technical knowledge level.

### "What's your warranty on installation?"
Proves whether they stand behind their work or disappear after final payment.

### "What percentage of your business is referrals?"
High referral rates indicate satisfied customers. Low rates suggest... well, you can figure it out.
